AI Tool Use

How AI helped:

AI helped create the responsive Bootstrap pages and shared CSS, add the sample tutor table and accessible request form, and write the PHP confirmation response. It also helped check that the pages link to the stylesheet and that key form and table requirements are present. I reviewed the generated content as part of building the project.
